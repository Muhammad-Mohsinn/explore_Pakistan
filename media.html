Hunza: The Valley That Stops Time

Surrounded by Rakaposhi, Ultar Sar and Lady Finger, Hunza is where apricot orchards climb up to glaciers. Visit Baltit Fort, cross the Attabad Lake by boat and drive the Karakoram Highway, one of the highest paved roads on Earth.

Spring blossoms and autumn gold are the two seasons that photographers wait for all year.

Travel Essentials

  • Valid CNIC or passport
  • Layered warm clothing
  • Power bank and offline maps
  • Comfortable trekking shoes
  • Sunscreen and sunglasses
